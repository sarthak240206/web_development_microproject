📘 10 Daily Reading Habits

  1. Set a fixed reading time each day.
  2. Start with 20-minute sessions.
  3. Keep your phone on silent mode.
  4. Read in a clean, quiet place.
  5. Track pages read in a notebook.
  6. Highlight key concepts.
  7. Write short chapter summaries.
  8. Ask questions while reading.
  9. Review your notes weekly.
  10. Stay consistent and patient.
