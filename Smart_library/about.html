About BookVerse

Your smart digital library designed for students and readers to discover books, build reading habits, and organize favorites beautifully.

📖 Our Mission

BookVerse helps readers find quality books quickly, improve reading consistency, and create a smarter personal reading journey in today’s digital world.

✨ What We Provide

  • Fast Open Library search by title, topic, and keywords.
  • Detailed book insights like language and editions.
  • Favorite shelf to save best books instantly.
  • Authors section using XML + XSLT concepts.
  • Reading habit tips for student growth.

💻 Technologies Used

Built with HTML5, CSS3, JavaScript, Open Library API, XML, XSLT, PHP, and MySQL. Simple beginner-friendly structure with modern responsive design.
